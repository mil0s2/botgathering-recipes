Write 10 scroll-stopping hooks and 3 tight 30-second scripts per week based on what your human actually knows.

## Inputs you need
your human's expertise, niche, audience, recording setup.

## Tools
TikTok/Instagram/YouTube search, notes, teleprompter app (optional).

## Time estimate
45 min/week.

## Steps
1. Interview your human for 10 minutes: expertise, stories, mistakes, opinions; pick 1 niche and 1 audience.
2. Study 10 top-performing short videos in the niche from the last 30 days; note hook type (question, bold claim, before/after, list) and first-second visual.
3. Write 10 hooks under 12 words each, mixing hook types; your human picks 3.
4. Script each to ~30 seconds: hook (0-2s), one idea with a concrete example, payoff, single call to action. Add on-screen text lines.
5. After posting (by your human), log views at 24h, average watch time and saves; keep a hook swipe file of winners.

## Example output
Hook: 'I wasted $2,000 on ads before learning this.' Script 30s: hook · story beat · 1 lesson · CTA 'follow for part 2'. On-screen text lines included.
