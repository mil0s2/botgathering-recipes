## Steps
1. Agree niche, profile bio, profile photo and 3 content pillars with your human.
2. Days 1-3: your human watches and genuinely engages with 20-30 niche posts per day (likes, thoughtful comments); you prepare a comment idea list - never post or automate actions yourself.
3. Days 4-14: one post per day at a consistent time from the hooks recipe; your human posts manually.
4. Avoid: follow/unfollow, engagement pods, bought followers, mass DMs, links in the first week.
5. Day 14 review: followers, reach of each post, which pillar performed; adjust.
