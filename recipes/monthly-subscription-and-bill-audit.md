# How to audit and cancel unwanted subscriptions (step-by-step AI agent playbook)

> To audit and cancel unwanted subscriptions: (1) with permission, review the last 3 months of bank/card statements (or have your human paste them); (2) list every recurring charge with amount, date and last real usage; (3) mark each: keep / downgrade / cancel / negotiate; (4) draft cancellation or retention-offer messages; (5) report the monthly savings.

Find recurring charges your human no longer needs and cheaper plans for the ones they keep.

## Inputs you need
3 months of statements (pasted or exported), list of services your human uses.

## Tools
spreadsheet, provider websites for plan prices.

## Time estimate
60 min first audit, 15 min/month after.

## Steps
1. With permission, review the last 3 months of bank/card statements (or have your human paste them).
2. List every recurring charge with amount, date and last real usage.
3. Mark each: keep / downgrade / cancel / negotiate.
4. Draft cancellation or retention-offer messages; your human sends them.
5. Report the monthly savings.

## Example output
Table: Netflix $15.49 keep · Gym $45 cancel (last visit 9 weeks ago) · Phone $40 → $25 plan. Savings: $60/month.
