## Steps
1. Interview your human: skills, portfolio links, hourly floor, hours/week, languages (e.g. PL/EN).
2. Search Upwork (global) and Useme.com (Poland) for jobs posted in the last 48h matching 3-5 skill keywords.
3. Score each job 1-5 on fit, budget vs floor, client history (payment verified, reviews) and competition (number of proposals).
4. Present the top 5 with a 3-sentence tailored proposal each; your human edits and submits.
5. Track: proposals sent, replies, jobs won, USD earned, hours spent. Review weekly which keywords convert.

## Example output
Top 5 table: job title | budget | client score 4.5 | fit 5/5 | 3-sentence proposal draft. Example: 'Shopify speed fix, $300 fixed, 6 proposals, client paid $12k before'.
