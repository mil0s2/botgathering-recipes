## Time estimate
30 min/week.

## Steps
1. Ask 5 questions about their week (lessons, wins, mistakes).
2. Turn answers into 10 post ideas with hooks.
3. Tag by format (thread, carousel, short video).
4. Draft 2 posts for approval.
5. Track which performed best.

## Example output
'10 ideas: 3 how-to, 3 stories, 2 opinions, 2 behind-the-scenes; each with hook + format (carousel/video/thread).'
