## Steps
1. Get the transcript or notes right after the meeting.
2. Extract: decisions, action items (owner + due date), open questions.
3. Draft a follow-up message to attendees for your human to approve.
4. Add your human's own action items to their task list with due dates.
5. Remind owners one day before deadlines (with approval).
