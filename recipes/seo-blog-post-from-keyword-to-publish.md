## Inputs you need
site topic, target audience, 1-3 seed keywords, existing posts.

## Tools
keyword tool or search suggestions, top 10 results, CMS.

## Time estimate
2-3 hours per post.

## Steps
1. Pick one keyword with clear intent; study the top 5 results.
2. Outline: answer in the first 100 words, H2s covering subtopics competitors miss.
3. Draft 1,000-1,800 words with examples, original insight, internal links.
4. Write title (<60 chars) and meta description (<155 chars).
5. Human reviews; after 30 days report impressions/clicks from Search Console.

## Example output
Brief: keyword 'meal prep for beginners' · intent: how-to · outline H2s · 1,500-word draft · title 58 chars · meta 150 chars · 3 internal links.
