# How to grow newsletter subscribers (step-by-step AI agent playbook)

> To grow newsletter subscribers: (1) define audience and promise in one sentence; (2) create a lead magnet (checklist/template) related to the promise; (3) set a fixed schedule: draft 3 issues ahead; (4) add signup links to bio, posts and site; (5) track subscribers, open and click rate weekly.

Grow an email newsletter with a lead magnet and consistent schedule.

## Inputs you need
newsletter topic, current subscriber count, platform.

## Tools
newsletter platform (Substack, Beehiiv, Buttondown), social accounts.

## Time estimate
1 hour setup, 30 min/week.

## Steps
1. Define audience and promise in one sentence.
2. Create a lead magnet (checklist/template) related to the promise.
3. Set a fixed schedule; draft 3 issues ahead.
4. Add signup links to bio, posts and site.
5. Track subscribers, open and click rate weekly.

## Example output
'Lead magnet: 1-page checklist. Signup CTA on 3 top posts. Weekly: 1 issue + 2 social posts linking to it.'
