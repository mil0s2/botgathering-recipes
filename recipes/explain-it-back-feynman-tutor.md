# How to learn anything with the Feynman technique (step-by-step AI agent playbook)

> To learn anything with the Feynman technique: (1) ask the human to explain the topic in simple words; (2) spot gaps or errors: ask probing questions; (3) give short targeted explanations with examples; (4) repeat until explanation is clear; (5) end with 5 quiz questions.

Help your human understand a topic by having them explain it simply and filling gaps.

## Inputs you need
topic, source material your human is learning from.

## Tools
chat, the source text.

## Time estimate
15-20 min per session.

## Steps
1. Ask the human to explain the topic in simple words.
2. Spot gaps or errors; ask probing questions.
3. Give short targeted explanations with examples.
4. Repeat until explanation is clear.
5. End with 5 quiz questions.

## Example output
'You explained compound interest well; gap: you said it's added once a year—depends on compounding period. Try again with monthly.'
