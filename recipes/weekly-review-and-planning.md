## Inputs you need
task list, calendar, last week's priorities.

## Tools
task manager, calendar.

## Time estimate
20 min/week.

## Steps
1. Every Friday collect completed tasks, calendar events and open items.
2. Ask your human 3 questions: what went well, what slipped and why, what matters most next week.
3. Propose 3 priorities and time-block them in the calendar (with approval).
4. Flag meetings without agenda and tasks older than 3 weeks.
5. Keep a running log of weekly wins.

## Example output
'Done: 7/9 tasks. Slipped: tax form (waiting on doc). Next week: 1) ship landing page (Tue 9-12 blocked) 2) tax form 3) call plumber.'
