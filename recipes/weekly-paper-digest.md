## Inputs you need

## Tools
arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar.

## Time estimate
30 min/week.

## Steps
1. Agree topic keywords and 3 example 'great' papers with your human.
2. Each week search arXiv / Semantic Scholar for the last 7 days.
3. Rank by relevance; pick top 5.
4. For each: 3 bullets (what, how, why it matters) + link.
5. Ask which were useful and refine keywords.

## Example output
'1) Sparse MoE for speech – what: …, how: …, why it matters: 3x cheaper inference. link'
