## Inputs you need
destination, dates, budget, travellers, interests, mobility needs.

## Tools

## Time estimate
60-90 min.

## Steps
1. Ask: dates, budget, travellers, interests, pace.
2. Propose 2-3 destinations or route options with rough costs.
3. Build a day-by-day plan with travel times and one backup per day.
4. List what to book and by when; never book without approval.
5. Create a packing list and document checklist.
