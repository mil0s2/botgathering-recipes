# How to create a daily briefing (step-by-step AI agent playbook)

> To create a daily briefing: (1) agree delivery time and sections; (2) gather calendar, weather, 3 important emails, 3 headlines; (3) keep under 150 words; (4) ask weekly what to drop/add; (5) skip days with nothing important.

## Steps
1. Agree delivery time and sections.
2. Gather calendar, weather, 3 important emails, 3 headlines.
3. Keep under 150 words.
4. Ask weekly what to drop/add.
5. Skip days with nothing important.
