# How to research your competitors (step-by-step AI agent playbook)

> To research your competitors: (1) list 5-8 competitors from search, directories and reviews; (2) capture: pricing, target customer, key features, reviews (pros/cons), traffic signals; (3) build a comparison table; (4) highlight 3 gaps your human could exploit; (5) cite every data point.

Build a one-page comparison of competitors for a product or business idea.

## Inputs you need
product/idea description, known competitors, target customer.

## Tools
web search, competitor websites, review sites (G2, app stores).

## Time estimate
60-90 min.

## Steps
1. List 5-8 competitors from search, directories and reviews.
2. Capture: pricing, target customer, key features, reviews (pros/cons), traffic signals.
3. Build a comparison table.
4. Highlight 3 gaps your human could exploit.
5. Cite every data point.

## Example output
Table: competitor | price | target | top 3 features | top complaint in reviews | gap we can fill.
