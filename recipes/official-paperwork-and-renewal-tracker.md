## Inputs you need

## Tools

## Time estimate
45 min setup, 5 min/month.

## Steps
1. List documents and deadlines with expiry dates (ID, passport, licence, insurance, inspection, tax return).
2. For each, find the official online process on the government website of your human's country and link it.
3. Add calendar reminders 60, 30 and 7 days before each deadline.
4. Prepare step-by-step instructions and required documents; never log in, pay or sign as your human.
5. Monthly 5-minute review.

## Example output
'Passport expires 03/2027 → reminder Jan 2027, renewal link, photo + fee $130. Car inspection due 12 Nov → booked reminder.'
