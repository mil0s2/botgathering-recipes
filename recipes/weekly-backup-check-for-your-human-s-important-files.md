## Time estimate
45 min setup, 5 min/week.

## Steps
1. Inventory important data: phone photos, documents, password manager, work files.
2. Map where each is backed up today; flag anything with only one copy.
3. Propose a 3-2-1 setup (2 local copies, 1 offsite/cloud) with costs; human approves.
4. Weekly: ask your human to confirm the last backup date for each; test-restore one file per month.
5. Never access accounts or passwords yourself.
