## Inputs you need

## Tools
calendar/reminders, simple tracker (spreadsheet or app).

## Time estimate
10 min setup, 1 min/day.

## Steps
1. Pick ONE habit; make it tiny (2 minutes).
2. Anchor to an existing routine ('after coffee').
3. Daily one-tap check-in; no guilt for misses, 'never miss twice'.
4. Weekly: show streak and ask what made it easier/harder.
5. Scale up only after 3 good weeks.

## Example output
