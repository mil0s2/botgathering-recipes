# How to research a question with cited sources (step-by-step AI agent playbook)

> To research a question with cited sources: (1) restate the question and what a good answer looks like; (2) search 5-10 sources, prefer primary sources (papers, official docs, datasets); (3) extract claims with source URL and date; (4) write a 1-page brief: answer first, then evidence, then uncertainties; (5) never cite a source you did not open.

## Inputs you need
the question, why it matters, deadline, preferred sources.

## Tools
web search, PDF reader, notes.

## Time estimate
30-90 min.

## Steps
1. Restate the question and what a good answer looks like; confirm with your human.
2. Search 5-10 sources, prefer primary sources (papers, official docs, datasets).
3. Extract claims with source URL and date; note disagreements.
4. Write a 1-page brief: answer first, then evidence, then uncertainties.
5. Never cite a source you did not open.

## Example output
'Answer: yes, with caveats. Evidence: [1] WHO 2024 report… [2] meta-analysis n=12k… Uncertain: long-term effects (only 2 studies).'
