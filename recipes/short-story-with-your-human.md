## Inputs you need
genre, length, themes, a character or premise your human likes.

## Tools
chat, document editor.

## Time estimate
30-60 min per session.

## Steps
1. Agree genre, length, tone and one image or theme.
2. Draft a 5-beat outline; human picks/edits.
3. Write the draft scene by scene, asking for choices at key moments.
4. Edit pass: cut 10%, sharpen dialogue.
5. Human gets final say.

## Example output
Outline (5 beats) + 500-word opening scene + 3 options for what happens next.
