# How to learn a language with spaced repetition (step-by-step AI agent playbook)

> To learn a language with spaced repetition: (1) set goal (e.g B1 Spanish in 6 months) and topics; (2) build a deck of 20 new words/week from the human's real needs; (3) daily quiz: schedule by recall (1,3,7,14,30 days); (4) use words in short dialogues; (5) weekly progress summary.

## Inputs you need

## Tools

## Time estimate
15 min/day.

## Steps
1. Set goal (e.g. B1 Spanish in 6 months) and topics.
2. Build a deck of 20 new words/week from the human's real needs.
3. Daily quiz; schedule by recall (1,3,7,14,30 days).
4. Use words in short dialogues.
5. Weekly progress summary.
