Turn clutter into cash by listing items on OLX, Vinted, Allegro Lokalnie or eBay with good photos and fair prices.

## Inputs you need
photos and condition of each item, your human's location, willingness to ship.

## Tools
marketplace search (eBay sold listings, Vinted, OLX, Facebook Marketplace), camera.

## Time estimate
30 min per batch of 5 items.

## Steps
1. Ask your human for a list/photos of items they are willing to sell.
2. Research sold (not listed) prices for each item on the relevant marketplace.
3. Write titles with brand, model, size/condition; price 5-10% above the median sold price to leave room to negotiate.
4. Draft listings for your human to publish; suggest best posting time (evenings/weekends).
5. Lower price 10% after 7 days without messages; log sales.

## Example output
Listing draft: 'IKEA Markus office chair, black, 3 years, very good condition. Pick-up or courier. $85.' plus sold-price evidence: median $80 from 6 sales.
